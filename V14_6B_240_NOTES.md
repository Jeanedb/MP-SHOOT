# V240 — West Service Landing

Rebuilt the west return-catwalk intersection as a covered landing at the same elevation as the service corridor. Replaced projecting bunker wall ends with a continuous rock enclosure, removed rails crossing the walking route, preserved the outer catwalk guardrail, and added a landing light and flush directional sign. Maintenance remains accessible through its existing gated entrance.

Validation: sampled walking clearance through the bend, two-way bullet occlusion on enclosure walls, pathfinding from catwalk to return corridor, mission terminal sequence through the elevator, and browser rendering checks across all five floors.
