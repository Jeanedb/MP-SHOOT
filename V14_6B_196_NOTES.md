# V14.6B.196 — Fading Corrections

Changed the Crime Reader's correction-lane presentation to thin glowing red lines that smoothly fade in and out, with a red rejection X at each lane's centre. The X appears sooner and fades later than the lines so it remains readable. Faint boundary lines still show the actual danger width. The old flicker and abrupt orange filled-lane flash are removed.

The three fixed lane layouts, warning duration, activation, damage and one-hit-per-lane behavior are unchanged. Paperwork bursts, pillar cover and the boss phases are retained.

Validation: Chromium rendering inspection and checks of fade endpoints, fade-in/out progression, X visibility, red color, original warning timing, damage and single-hit behavior. No JavaScript or WebGL errors in those checks.
