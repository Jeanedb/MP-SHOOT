# V14.6B.228 — Crawler Hunt

Wall crawlers use a sideways climbing pose and a smaller body-to-rock gap, with short claw supports connecting the body to the surface. Ceiling poses and the approved gory artwork remain.

Crawlers still begin with an ambush. Seeing the player or taking damage permanently engages them for that encounter. After a strike they seek new nearby wall or ceiling perches, scoring reachable positions for flank/rear placement relative to player facing. Retreat no longer sends them to their original spawn perch. They remain within authored cave territories, avoiding pursuits into unrelated rooms or unsupported bridge voids. The exposed rock outcrop offers wall perches only; enclosed caves offer both wall and ceiling choices.

Relocations travel continuously rather than teleporting, check sightlines before selection and during movement, and cannot damage the player. Crawlers pause after attachment and warn before their next attack. Taser/bullet dislodging, LIDAR hold, gory corpse fall, attack damage, accepted sounds and other enemies are preserved.

Validation: browser tests exercise five cave placements, overhead aiming, cover, pounce warning/dodge/damage, taser knockdown, LIDAR corpse hold/fall, relocation to a different attached surface, persistent engagement after retreat, and all five floors rendering without browser/WebGL errors. Wall and ceiling captures reviewed.
