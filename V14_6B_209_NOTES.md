# V14.6B.209 — Police Operations Full Route

Extends Floor 5 beyond the original cavern crossing into Logistics, equipment storage, planning and briefing offices, and Restricted Operations Control.

## Playable route

- Establish the far-side field link, clear Logistics, and collect the equipment authorization.
- Secure deployment authority in the planning wing. A pristine office sits beside a damaged structural inspection area.
- Open the service catwalk and return across a different part of the cavern to the War Room.
- Clear the returning reserve detail and report the authorization to unlock Restricted Operations.
- Secure the control room and initiate an eight-second descent override. Preplaced response teams enter through side shutters; surviving responders prevent completion.
- Board and activate the departure elevator. The debrief explicitly identifies Floor 6 as not built yet and the current campaign endpoint.

The WO remains unseen. This is an organized final defence, not a new boss fight. All 27 enemies are placed at level construction; encounter triggers activate them and open access rather than creating visible reinforcements.

- Normal campaign play, Floor 4 inventory carryover, Floor 5 save/resume and retry support, and a full developer playtest entry.

- Basic furnishing, supplies, utility equipment, and environmental storytelling across the extension. Further atmosphere and encounter tuning can follow playtesting.
