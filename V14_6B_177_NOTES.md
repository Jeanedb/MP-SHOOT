# V14.6B.177 — Production Floor

The Return Routing Plant now requires work inside the machinery hall, with a larger central combat clearing and stronger visual separation between surfaces. Download `MP_SHOOT_V14_6B_177_PRODUCTION_FLOOR.html`, open locally, then choose **Mission Select → Floor 4 · Crime Reader Playtest**.

## Gameplay changes

After attaching the Will Say, enter the plant and **set the manual diverter on the production floor**. It stands more than ten metres inside the hall and can be operated under fire. Then reach the north-east booth, clear the plant contacts and **release the returned file**. Only that release opens the permanent shortcut and permits GO resubmission. Objectives, notices and the map marker follow these two actions.

The west maintenance walk is divided into three separate pockets; the east walk is divided into two. Each pocket reconnects to the hall rather than providing a continuous perimeter route. Service trunks also interrupt the uninterrupted lane immediately inside the western wall. There are still tactical flanks and shelter, but the central diverter cannot be completed from the perimeter.

Three sorting banks are removed, leaving six and opening the area around the stamping press. The wider clearing supports cross-room fighting, movement around the hazard and longer sightlines. The press and both automatic shutters retain their existing warnings, timing and occupied-zone safety. A route through the hall remains usable with both shutters closed. Additional supplies reward entering the clearing.

## Presentation changes

- Warm pale concrete walls, a blue-grey floor, olive machines and brighter yellow safety markings make the room easier to read.
- Local pools of light lift the conveyor crossings while the high roof remains darker.
- Sorting banks gain angled intake hoods, cylindrical rollers, moving feed packets, compressor units and ducts connected to the overhead tracks.
- The diverter has a visible lever and indicator that change when the return line is selected.

The **6.8 m hall ceiling** is retained. Subdividing the maintenance routes increases the plan to **63 spaces**, while its footprint remains **8,052.68 m²**. The submission machine, other environmental rooms, boss mechanics, gold-card access, secrets and original elevator departure are preserved. No gameplay music or embedded media is added.

## Validation

The full correction sequence, both routing actions, all-room connectivity, boss fight and elevator departure passed browser testing. Targeted checks verify physical pocket partitions, the absence of a continuous maintenance bypass, diverter and release requirements, clear control approaches and the return shortcut with both shutters closed, intake animation, pause/map behavior and replay resource cleanup.
