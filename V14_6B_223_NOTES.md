# V14.6B.223 — Enemy Discharge Tuning

A small adjustment to the accepted V222 enemy launch sound, making it easier to distinguish from the player's pistol.

- Reduced the low body tone and bass component of enemy ranged launches.
- Softened the initial high-frequency attack and added a brief filtered electrical fizz after it.
- Slightly extended the discharge tail while retaining heavy/grenadier/officer differences. No pitched sci-fi sweep was reintroduced.
- Death grunts, player pain, impacts, preferred pistol/C6 reports and all gameplay/visual tuning are retained.

Validation: generated-buffer and offline mix checks, launch/death/damage event checks, no legacy pre-shot aiming tones, audio lifecycle/voice caps, and all-five-floor update/render checks. Pistol/C6 reports, weapon profiles/placement and the LIDAR hold match the previous build. Perceived distinction and final mix require a player listening test.
