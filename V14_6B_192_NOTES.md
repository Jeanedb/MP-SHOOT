# V14.6B.192 — Enemy Arrivals and Return Routes

Open `MP_SHOOT_V14_6B_192_ENEMY_ARRIVALS.html`.

Ordinary room encounters now occupy their rooms before the player arrives. Seven authored Floor 1 garrison groups, ten Floor 3 groups (including the Review Pit) and sixteen Floor 4 room groups plus its initial plant garrison are placed during level loading. Existing room/phase thresholds activate their existing actors rather than creating duplicates. These guards retain normal idle, sight and combat behavior, so a player who can see or engage a room before crossing its old trigger can do so. Previously killed garrison members do not respawn at the threshold.

Later ordinary reinforcements use delayed admission. A spawn point must be clear, sufficiently distant, hidden by opaque cover at multiple heights and across the body width, and connected to the player by a currently navigable route. Recent player positions are checked as well as the current position. Transparent glass does not count as concealment. Candidate adjustments remain near the authored position and within its room where room boundaries are available. If no suitable point exists, the response waits; there is no visible timeout fallback. Checks are staggered with a bounded navigation-search budget.

Waiting actors are encounter records, not invisible enemy entities. They cannot be rendered or targeted, and required encounter-clear checks include the waiting records so a terminal or lockdown cannot release prematurely. Admission pauses with menus/maps and resets on level entry or replay. Floor 3's existing archive ambush now checks body concealment rather than only its center. Floor 2 retains its existing eleven preplaced guards. Its alarmed, staggered hatch reinforcements start above the ceiling and descend into view over 0.95 seconds; confinement repair no longer visibly teleports a guard.

Floor 4 retains its first review-return response and adds two finite two-enemy responses: a Shooter/Flanker response after collecting RED following disclosure, approaching through the cleared cross corridor, and a Shooter/Charger response after collecting the final summons authorization, using the cleared investigation approach. They investigate the returning player's route. Unadmitted optional responses expire when their correction stage ends or the boss starts; already present actors are not despawned. Quiet branches, existing shortcut gates and museum rooms remain available. One finite C8 ammunition bundle is added to the administrative spine to support the extra combat; it does not refill between returns.

Boss mechanics, arenas and health are unchanged for this first pass. All eight enemy artwork sheets are byte-identical to V191, and all V191 menu, collection and audio changes are included.

Validation: controlled Chromium checks verify occupied rooms before thresholds, activation without duplicate actors, direct-view and glass rejection, waiting-encounter accounting, pause/replay/cross-floor reset, concealed reachable admission, and actual advancement of both added response groups. All four added actors passed live body-visibility checks at creation. Floor 1 Patrol staging, Floor 2 hatch spawning/descent, and Floor 3 Review Pit gating were checked. The full Floor 4 correction/factory/boss/elevator route passes with delayed arrivals included. Floors 1–3 geometry, collision, doors, items, IDs and supplies match V191; Floor 2's original guard positions/types/tags also match. Screenshots review the occupied investigation room and return corridor. No JavaScript errors were observed.

These checks establish arrival visibility and progression. Ordinary play should assess how early room engagement and the two added returns affect combat pressure. V191 remains available separately.
