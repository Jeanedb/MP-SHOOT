# V14.6B.173 — Administrative Excellence

Floor 4 now has a more distinct institutional personality: ceremonial administrative perfection alongside an exhausting correction process. Boss artwork remains the V172 placeholder pending the planned sprites.

Download `MP_SHOOT_V14_6B_173_ADMINISTRATIVE_EXCELLENCE.html`, open it locally, then choose **Mission Select → Floor 4 · Crime Reader Playtest**. E interacts, M opens the map, and O recalls the objective.

## Environmental additions

- **Museum of Administrative Excellence:** a new 18 × 14 m room beside the north staff return passage, with two entrances. Glass cases exhibit the ceremonial red pen, golden stapler, first rejected report, unused approval stamp and self-awarded lifetime trophy. A procedural portrait shows the Crime Reader shaking hands with himself. These are ordinary, static exhibits.
- **Wall of Shame:** the existing Observation Gallery now displays four annotated rejected reports and an empty frame reserved for CPL Brock T. Magnum, marked PENDING REVIEW. The blue-card placement remains intact.
- **Approved Terminology:** the QA display replaces the climbing statistics with approved euphemisms. An accompanying notice demands concise, complete reporting while warning against unnecessary detail and omissions.
- **Complaints:** an optional, repeatable shredder at File Intake accepts a complaint, runs a short paper-and-sound cycle, then reports COMPLAINT SUCCESSFULLY RESOLVED. It does not change mission progress.
- **Submission machinery:** the existing File Intake submission point is surrounded by rollers, gears, a hydraulic assembly, inspection glass, pipes and warning lights. Its processing display reports completed checks, 0.00% reviewer satisfaction and unspecified deficiencies. The original submission timing, requirements and correction reasons remain in place; there is no additional mandatory machine.
- **Perfection Chamber:** the existing S1 secret is expanded into a sterile, symmetrical room. Look for the 0 ERRORS precision panel in Records. Inspecting the sole approved investigation opens its folder to reveal a blank interior: NO ERRORS IDENTIFIED. NO INVESTIGATION CONDUCTED. An armour reward remains optional. The floor still has two secrets.

## Presentation and scope

The senior corridor and boss-suite breaches now have irregular recessed openings, charred edges, exposed structure and rubble. These are visual wall damage, with reinforced-wall collision retained. Boss attack warnings use proofreader crosses, boundary traces and scanning marks; their damage and timing are unchanged.

The hidden correction-return sequence, key-card gates, boss mechanics and return to the original elevator are preserved. Floors 1–3 are unchanged. No gameplay music or embedded media was added.

Floor 4 contains **50 spaces and approximately 5,935 m²** of enclosed area, within the existing **95.4 × 101.6 m** envelope. The museum adds 252 m² and the secret expansion adds 35 m². The HTML grows by approximately 27 KB over V172.
