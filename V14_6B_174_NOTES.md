# V14.6B.174 — Dedicated Departments

The Floor 4 environmental features now occupy separate rooms. Four new enclosed rooms add **424 m²** to V173, bringing the floor to **6,358.92 m²**, with **54 spaces** and a **107.4 × 101.6 m** overall envelope. V173 remains available separately.

Download `MP_SHOOT_V14_6B_174_DEDICATED_DEPARTMENTS.html`, open locally, then choose **Mission Select → Floor 4 · Crime Reader Playtest**.

## New rooms

| Room | Size | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wall of Shame Gallery | 12 × 8 m / 96 m² | New room east of the original Observation Gallery |
| Department of Approved Terminology | 12 × 14 m / 168 m² | New room east of the museum |
| Complaints Resolution Department | 10 × 8 m / 80 m² | New room west of File Intake |
| Crime Review Automation Plant | 10 × 8 m / 80 m² | New room north of File Intake |

The framed rejected reports and empty Brock frame move into the dedicated Wall of Shame. The terminology display and contradictory reporting instructions move out of QA and into their own department, with reference tables. Complaints has its own waiting seats, policy notices and working shredder.

The existing GO submission interaction moves into the Automation Plant. Its machinery is enlarged horizontally, with additional rear equipment, side cabinets and overhead pipes. An eight-metre-wide machine base occupies more than half the small room, leaving a narrow front approach to the controls. Objectives and submission notices identify the new location. The authorization return tray and gold-card collection remain at File Intake.

The **Museum of Administrative Excellence** and **Perfection Chamber** retain their dedicated rooms from V173. No original department is replaced by the four new rooms. The Observation Gallery retains blue-card collection; QA retains its workstations and local correction control.

## Behavior and validation

The correction requirements, review delay, surprise returns, key-card gates, boss mechanics and departure through the original elevator are preserved. The complaint shredder remains optional and repeatable. Machinery animations, paper particles and spatial audio follow the relocated equipment.

Browser testing passed the complete correction route, access gates, all-room connectivity, boss fight and elevator departure. Targeted checks passed the new-room entrances, relocated displays and controls, optional interactions, secret reward, pause behavior and replay reset. Visual checks reported no script or shader errors. Deterministic comparisons confirmed that Floors 1–3 geometry, collision, doors and item positions still match V172.

Boss artwork remains the temporary model pending the planned sprites. No music or embedded media was added.
